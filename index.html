How to Play Flip 7

The Objective

Be the first player to reach 200 points (or your selected target) across rounds by flipping cards, banking points, and pulling off the Flip 7 bonus!

Turn Decisions: Hit or Stay?

  • HIT: Flip 1 card face up from the deck. If it's a number card you already hold, you BUST! All cards in your hand score 0 points for the round.
  • STAY: Bank all card values in front of you. You safely sit out the rest of the round.

The Flip 7 Bonus (+15 pts!)

If you collect 7 unique number cards, the round ends immediately! You score all your cards plus an extra +15 bonus points!
